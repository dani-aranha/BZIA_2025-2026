Where is Canadian household money going?

THE STORY (this is the most important part)
You don't present the graph.
You present an interpretation of the graph.

Speech Structure (2 minutes)
1️⃣ Context (20 seconds)

“We've heard a lot about inflation and interest rates, but before discussing causes, it's important to look at how households actually spend their money over time.”

2️⃣ What the graph shows (40 seconds)

“Here I compare total household consumption with spending on food and non-alcoholic beverages.

What stands out is that spending on food grows consistently and keeps pace with—or in some periods grows faster than—total consumption.”

3️⃣ Key Insight (30 seconds)

“This suggests that an increasingly larger share of household budgets is going to essential goods, which reduces flexibility for other types of consumption.”

4️⃣ Connection to the project (20 seconds)

“This behavior helps explain why inflation and interest rates have such a strong impact on financial well-being: when fixed expenses increase, there is less room for savings or discretionary spending.”
