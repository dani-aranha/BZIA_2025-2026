
✅ Projeto – Summary of Requirements and Current Progress
Project Objective (as defined in the Project Charter)
The objective of this group project is to build a Power BI dashboard to understand how inflation and interest rate changes affect Canadian household consumption, income, and savings, with special attention to food spending, across:

Time
Regions (provinces)
Income and consumption categories

The final output is a story‑driven presentation, limited to approximately 10 minutes, with each group member presenting insights from their assigned perspective.

Project Requirements (as communicated by the group lead)

Clean and prepare the provided datasets.
Build approximately 5 key visuals for the final dashboard.
Each group member:

Works on one specific analytical angle
Creates one report page (tab) in Power BI
Shares their work for final compilation into a single .pbix file.


Focus on clear storytelling, not deep modeling or forecasting.
Avoid overlapping analysis between group members.

Key Insight Delivered

Food spending is a stable and unavoidable part of household budgets in Canada.
While its share of total consumption remains relatively constant over time, the level of pressure varies across provinces, helping explain why inflation and interest‑rate changes impact households differently depending on regional context.
